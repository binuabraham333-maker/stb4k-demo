STB4K Support

Help for the STB4K player on iPhone, iPad and Apple TV.

Contact

E-mail: stb4k.support@gmail.com
Or ask a question or report a problem here: github.com/binuabraham333-maker/stb4k-demo/issues (a free GitHub account is needed). Please write your device, the app version (Settings → About & device) and what happened. Never post your portal address, MAC address, user name or password.

STB4K does not provide channels or films. For questions about your subscription, channels or your account, contact your IPTV provider.

Add your portal

  1. Open STB4K and tap Portal, then Add portal.
  2. Choose the type your provider gave you: Stalker (MAG) (portal address and MAC), Xtream Codes (server, user name, password) or M3U playlist (a link, or on iPhone / iPad also a file).
  3. Type the details, tap Test Connection, then Save Provider.

You can also send the details from your phone over the same Wi-Fi: Portal → Add from phone (QR code), on the Apple TV, iPhone and iPad. On iPhone and iPad you can scan your provider's QR code too: Portal → Scan provider QR.

Common questions

"The portal does not accept this device for this MAC"
Your provider has to register this MAC address, or reset its device lock. If your provider gave you a device ID, enter it in Portal → Edit → Advanced.
A channel stays black
STB4K tries the other player by itself. You can also choose it in the player: ⋯ → Player 1 / Player 2.
Sound in the background
On iPhone and iPad the sound plays on when you leave the app or lock the screen. Use Play / Pause on the lock screen or in Control Center.
The Demo playlist
Free content made by stb4k to try the app (see details). It goes away by itself when you save your first own portal, or delete it in Portal with the bin button.
