Privacy Policy

STB4K for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV

Last updated: 26 October 2026
No data collectedNo accountNo analytics No trackingNo ads

1Introduction

STB4K is made by KoMbAn (Binu Abrahaem), Belgium. This policy explains how the STB4K apps for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV handle your information.

STB4K is a media player only. It does not provide, sell or include any channels, films or playlists; you add the service of your own provider.

2Information we collect

None. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking.

What you enter stays only on your own device:

  • The portals and playlists you add (address, MAC address, user name, password), kept in the app's own storage, with the password, the MAC address and your parental PIN in the system Keychain.
  • Your settings, favourites, recently watched channels and watch progress.
  • A backup file, only if you make one yourself, saved where you choose.

3Who the app connects to

  • Your own providers: the portals, playlists and stream servers you add. They receive what any player sends them, for example your IP address and the MAC address or login you entered. Their own privacy policies apply; we have no control over them.
  • The built-in Demo (iPhone and iPad): its playlist and videos are files on our GitHub Pages site, loaded only when you open the Demo.
  • A version file (min-version.json) on the same GitHub Pages site, read when the app starts or comes back on screen, to see whether an update is needed. The check is done on your device.

None of the requests to our GitHub Pages site contain any user data, device ID or account details.

4Camera, photos and local network

  • Camera: only to read a QR code with portal details from your provider, when you choose to. Nothing is recorded, stored or sent.
  • Photos: only a photo of a QR code that you pick yourself; the app gets no access to your library.
  • Local network: only to send portal details from your phone to the STB4K app on your Apple TV, iPhone or iPad, on the same Wi-Fi. Nothing goes to the internet.

5Third-party services

None. The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking services (no Firebase, no Google Analytics, no Facebook SDK). The video engine VLCKit (open source) plays the video on your device and sends nothing.

6Data retention and deletion

Everything the app keeps is on your device, for as long as you keep it there. You can delete a portal in the app at any time, and deleting the app removes its data from the device. We keep nothing, so there is nothing to delete on our side.

7Data security

Passwords are stored in the system Keychain, protected by your device. Nothing is stored on our servers. Connections to our GitHub Pages site use HTTPS; connections to your provider use whatever your provider offers.

8Children

We collect no data from anyone, including children. A parental PIN can lock adult categories, and you can hide any category you do not want to see.

9Your rights (GDPR and CCPA)

If you live in the European Economic Area (GDPR) or in California (CCPA), you have the right to access, correct or delete the personal data a company holds about you, and to know whether it is sold or shared. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct, delete, sell or share. You are still welcome to contact us with any question, and you may complain to your local data protection authority.

10Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
