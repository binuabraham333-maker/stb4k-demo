# stb4k Demo

**All demo content is made by stb4k.** Every video, sound, poster and subtitle in this repository was
generated by stb4k for the STB4K player (iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, Android). It contains no films, TV channels, music, sounds,
pictures or streams from anyone else.

Published with GitHub Pages:

- Playlist: https://binuabraham333-maker.github.io/stb4k-demo/demo.m3u
- Support: https://binuabraham333-maker.github.io/stb4k-demo/support.html
- Privacy Policy: https://binuabraham333-maker.github.io/stb4k-demo/privacy.html
- Minimum version: https://binuabraham333-maker.github.io/stb4k-demo/min-version.json — the app (build 35 and later)
  reads it at start; a lower build shows only "message" and an App Store button. Optional "url": the App Store link.
  Raise "minBuild" only when every user must update.

## What is in the Demo

Three live channels in the category **STB4K Demo Live** and four short films in the category **STB4K Demo**
(no series).

Live channels — the films below played again and again (6 hours per round of the list, then it starts over):

| Channel | Plays |
|---|---|
| STB4K Live 1 · Showcase | STB4K Showcase |
| STB4K Live 2 · Two Languages | STB4K Two Audio Languages (Audio: English / Malayalam) |
| STB4K Live 3 · 5.1 Surround | STB4K 5.1 Surround Test |

Films:

| Item | Length | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| STB4K Showcase | 0:30 | A short tour: Live TV, films and series, two players, sound and subtitles, ending on "Ready to play" |
| STB4K Two Audio Languages | 0:30 | Two audio tracks: English (one low beep a second) and Malayalam (two high beeps) |
| STB4K English Subtitles | 0:30 | An English subtitle track, a new line every five seconds |
| STB4K 5.1 Surround Test | 0:36 | 5.1 sound: Front Left, Front Right, Centre, Subwoofer, Surround Left, Surround Right in turn, each named on screen, then all together |

All videos are 1920 × 1080, H.264 with AAC sound. No logo and no slogan in any video. Each film opens with a title
card; its poster is a frame of that card.

## How it was made

- Pictures: FFmpeg's built-in colour gradients — no camera, no footage.
- Sounds: sine-wave beeps, tones and chords calculated by FFmpeg (`aevalsrc`) — no music or recordings.
- Text and title cards: drawn by `tools/render.swift` with the open font **Atkinson Hyperlegible**
  (© 2020 Braille Institute of America, SIL Open Font License 1.1, see `tools/Atkinson-Hyperlegible-OFL.txt`).
  The font files are not part of this repository; only pictures made with them are.
- The STB4K logo (`images/stb4k-logo.png`) belongs to stb4k.
- Everything can be built again with `tools/build4.py` (FFmpeg and Swift on a Mac; `tools/build.py` made the earlier Demo).
  The live channels (`live/`) are the same films copied into HLS pieces without re-encoding, by `tools/live4.py`.
