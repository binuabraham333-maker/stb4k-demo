STB4K Demo

A small, free and legal playlist so anyone can try the STB4K player without an IPTV subscription.

STB4K is a media player. It does not sell, provide or include TV channels or films. You add the playlist or portal your own provider gives you. When no portal is added yet, the app shows this Demo playlist:

https://binuabraham333-maker.github.io/stb4k-demo/demo.m3u

Everything in it is either an open movie released under a Creative Commons licence, or a public test stream published by its owner for testing video players.

Remove the demo

In STB4K open Portal, tap the bin next to Demo. It does not come back.
