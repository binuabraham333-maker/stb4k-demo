STB4K Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026. Applies to the STB4K apps for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV.

Summary

STB4K does not collect, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The developer does not receive any information about you or what you watch.

What stays on your device

  • The portals you add (address, MAC address, user name, password) — stored only on your device, in the app's own storage with a copy in the system Keychain.
  • Your settings, favourites, recently watched items and watch times — only on your device.
  • A backup file, only if you create one yourself.

Who the app connects to

  • The portals, playlists and stream servers you add. They receive what any player sends them (for example your IP address and the MAC address or login you entered). Their own privacy policies apply; STB4K has no control over them.
  • The built-in Demo playlist on GitHub Pages and the servers of its open movies and test streams (Internet Archive, Blender Foundation, Unified Streaming, Mux, Apple), only when you open the Demo.

Camera, photos and local network

The camera and photo library are used only to read a QR code with portal details, when you choose to. Nothing is recorded, stored or sent. The local network is used only to send portal details from your phone to your Apple TV.

Children

STB4K does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children. A PIN can lock adult categories.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page. Questions: github.com/binuabraham333-maker/stb4k-demo/issues.
